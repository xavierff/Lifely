# 1.52.0 — Autonomous Social Life

NPC-initiated invitations from actual people: milestone birthdays, five-year anniversaries of recorded player marriages, reunions, recurring friendship gatherings, personal celebrations and requests to talk. Two offers per year, one activity and quoted cost to attend; free polite decline; important ignored occasions can reduce close bonds by two, no penalty for cancelled or casual invitations. Reunions can create two-year traditions with real guests. NPC talk uses existing three-choice dialogue system. Saved bounded inbox/history, no new RNG draws during generation. Six core and one narrow browser regression. Build 62; CI pending. Invitations are annual abstractions, not a real-time calendar or full NPC wedding simulation.
