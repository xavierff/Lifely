# Lifely 1.36.0 — Business Empire & Financial Comebacks

Six independent acquisition targets, maximum four concurrent holdings, acquired with existing cash after starting a primary company. Each has separate quality, demand, competition, annual operating profits/losses and equity valuation. Investment, strategy, sale, distress, closure, and liquidity recovery decisions use ordinary actions. Distressed sales realize 60% rather than 85% of current equity. Annual profit enters taxable income once; equity enters net worth and estate liquidation once. A liquidity crisis blocks new acquisitions and encourages real debt repayment or a frugal budget. No automatic debt forgiveness or formal bankruptcy proceeding. Named competitors are simulated market pressures, not independent NPC entrepreneurs. Money dashboard, Chronicle and inherited history.
