# 1.63.0 — Advanced World Simulation & Connected Economy
Build 73. Six country-level economic records with separate activity, inflation, policy rates, housing indices, demand and stochastic disruptions/booms. The 18 existing city profiles locate actual companies and properties; property valuations use their country housing growth and new listings use destination cost scale. Ordinary player salaries incorporate regional activity, NPC living costs follow residence, and company operations use headquarters cost/salary and a diversified regional activity factor. Overseas branches spend existing company cash into operating capital, not an extra asset. Three board priorities trade company cash for quality, staff wellbeing or operating reserves. Optional competitive acquisition compares a player bid with a funded NPC offer; the winning buyer pays the existing owner. NPC investors trade pooled equity units with real savings, receive distributions, have their holdings reflected in existing wealth rankings, and liquidate the playable heir's existing holdings into cash on succession. Annual cash reconciliation compares opening and closing cash, isolates actual core cash movement, company receipts, property cash flow and investment receipts, and shows an explicit other residual for unclassified changes, alongside debt and net-worth change. No extra cash is created by the reporting process.
Limitations: country-level rather than independently calculated city indices, fictional shocks and external fund counterparties. Existing direct acquisition paths remain; no full auction order book. Individual venture equity, true global billionaire registry and autonomous branch hiring abroad are not added. Regional policy rates are reported; existing mortgage and global bond pricing retain their original algorithms. Cash reporting is reconciled but not a full tax/accounting overhaul. Existing legacy asset systems remain separate. Ten engine tests and one narrow browser regression authored, pending CI.
