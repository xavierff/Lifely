# 1.54.0 — Persistent Society & Multigenerational NPC Dynasties
Build 64. Builds 62 and 63 merged after passing CI.
- Stable person and household IDs across succession, including old players as historical identities rather than duplicate active NPCs.
- World clock continues at the deceased player's last year when a younger heir takes over. An heir's existing NPC spouse and children become the player family.
- Genealogy derived from actual family edges; parents, children, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, aunts/uncles, cousins, nieces/nephews and in-laws, with interactive selection and household references.
- Independent net estate settlement once per NPC death, distributed among surviving known spouse/children. NPC businesses use existing funds, have annual returns and can transfer to adult family heirs. If player takes ownership, value is transferred into cash once rather than duplicating a playable business.
- Family links can develop alliances and rivalries; retained household milestones, finances and social ties continue after succession.
- Existing saves supported. Independent family population limited to 64 generated people, 64 unions and 80 links. Recent estate/news history bounded, enterprise records capped at 24. Extended lineage grows with succession. Population outside household graph is retained up to a soft limit where possible.
- Annual simulation and simplified kinship/estate rules, not a full legal system. Business ownership converts to player cash; no separate inherited playable business management. NPCs continue using existing occupation and interaction systems; autonomous colleague matching and romantic selection from existing NPCs are not introduced here.
- Nine core tests and one mobile-browser regression added; CI pending.
