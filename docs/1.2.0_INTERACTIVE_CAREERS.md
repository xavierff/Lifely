# Lifely 1.2.0 (build 11) — career gameplay QA checklist

## Implementation scope
- Existing 1.1.0 tactical sports, film direction and business liveboard are retained with their world and cash outcomes.
- New fictional civic debate: 4 authored moderator topics, 12 distinct response choices, candidate/rival podium stage, support snapshot, credibility/community trust bars, two interactions per stage, random outcomes seeded from the life.
- New fictional clinical case: 4 stages, 12 authored care-team coordination options, a visual patient-case board, explicit documentation/safety, consent, team coordination and follow-up meters. No treatment or diagnosis instructions.
- Named collaborators persist decisions in campaign reactions, character history and the Work hub. Rivalry, respect and/or trust change.
- Every completed career creates aftermath chapters at +1, +3, +5 simulated years. They affect the existing sport, entertainment, business, civic or knowledge stats and appear in the journal. Each chapter allows an optional same-year response using one of the player's three yearly activities, and cannot block age progression.
- Original tiny mono WAV samples (tap, success, setback) are bundled locally; effect sounds default to On with a toggle saved outside character slots. The in-game animation respects OS Reduce Motion.
- Simulation schema remains version 2; all new persisted fields optional for old saves. Active projects, old portfolio records and dynasty transitions stay compatible.

## Automated verification commands
- `npm test` — previous engine regression suite plus new playfield, NPC, years-later and inheritance checks.
- `npx expo install --check` — SDK 58 dependency alignment.
- `npx expo export --platform all` — iOS, Android and web bundles.
- `npm run test:e2e` — browser interaction of both new boards, audio settings, persistence, narrow widths and returning career chapter actions.
- The GitHub workflow `.github/workflows/checks.yml` runs the above. Look up the actual result before claiming a pass for build 11.

## Device test gates (not yet verified)
1. On a small iPhone and a compact Android device, enter sports, film, civic, medical and business projects, check each visual control remains at least 44 pt high, and confirm text wraps properly.
2. Play through every project with positive and negative routes. Save and force quit after round 1 and 3, then reload. Confirm two interactions per stage, no duplicate payments and no softlocks even with cash at zero.
3. Confirm the sound toggle works on physical iOS/Android, including iOS silent switch and when the application regains focus. Verify no microphone permission is requested.
4. Test Reduce Motion, VoiceOver/TalkBack, dark mode, tablet orientation and screen-reader announcements.
5. Advance ages +1, +3 and +5 for all projects. Confirm the follow-up invitation appears only during its relevant year, can be answered once, or can be ignored without blocking.
6. Confirm completed story history persists, contacts retain relationships, and selecting an heir does not transfer ancestor-only obligations.
7. Run 20 human sessions (at least 10 inexperienced life-sim players) and measure completion, perceived choice quality, reading time, enjoyment and repeat engagement before any competitor superiority claim.

## Safety and store gates
Election results and medicine are intentionally fictional abstraction and do not represent real voting procedures or clinical treatment. App Store and Play Store releases still require signed device builds, store content review, privacy/support pages, accessibility verification and independent playtests. The new 1.2.0 version/build code is not itself a signed release.
