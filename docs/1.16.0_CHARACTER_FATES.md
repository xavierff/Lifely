# Lifely 1.16.0 — Character Fates & Divergent Futures (Build 26)

This release expands original character-driven gameplay without replacing 1.15.0 reunions, the Life Director, autonomous NPCs, or established financial/career mechanics.

## Player experience
- **New optional three-chapter Fate Threads** with actual persistent NPCs: a friendship in jeopardy, a respected rivalry, a shared ambition, or a family promise. Chapters unfold over several years with choices to care, compete, or set boundaries.
- **Surprising story turns** are selected deterministically per character and story; second/third chapters react to actual NPC trust changes accumulated while the player was apart, not made-up offscreen events.
- **Real gameplay consequences**: player wellbeing/knowledge/confidence and NPC bonds, trust, and rivalry change at each selected decision. Different paths can end in lifelong alliance, respected rivalry, reconciliation, distance, estrangement, or an interrupted/unanswered story.
- **Clearer emotional presentation**: character spotlight portraits, three-part chapter progress rail, connection/rivalry/hope meters, trade-offs beside every choice, animated response feedback that respects reduced-motion accessibility settings, and sound effects using the existing opt-in preference.
- **Consequences visible later** in the Life overview, People Fate Story library, the character-story director's context, the Life Chronicle, and descendants' ancestral story recaps.

## Design and compatibility
A new optional fate object in the existing v2 save schema records one active thread, up to 24 completed threads, 32 updates and 12 ancestral recaps. It is validated without rewriting previous saves. Prior saved games remain valid when the object is absent.

Starting or continuing a chapter consumes one ordinary yearly action. Responding to the three story options has no additional action charge. Players cannot accidentally advance in the middle of a live scene, but waiting and ready follow-ups never block year advancement. A ready chapter unattended for five years ends as an unanswered possibility, without artificial stat penalties. Unavailable or deceased NPCs cannot be brought back as part of a story. Story selection and endings do not spend RNG or award unearned money.

## QA gates
The final PR must pass the complete engine regression suite, npx expo install --check, npx expo export --platform all, and the Playwright browser suite. Dedicated tests cover deterministic replay, real diverging NPC relationships, autonomous trust drift, missing participants, unattended stories, old saves, lineage, action costs, save validation, mobile widths and browser reloads. No signed binary, store submission or automation modification is part of this task.
