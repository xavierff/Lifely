# Lifely 1.29.0 — Institutional World Expansion

- Real groups accumulate strain, decline and recover according to cohesion, tension and standing.
- A primary group with four or more members may face a two-year faction challenge. Mediation, reform advocacy and neutrality are normal activities; autonomous outcomes continue without the player.
- A split creates a new organization with two actual members, leaving the original identity intact. Parent and breakaway may exchange non-leader members or reunite after genuine recovery. A reunited group becomes a dormant historical record; it is not deleted or silently repopulated.
- Maximum eight groups, at most one unmerged breakaway per primary group, and twelve relationship links. Parent and breakaway rosters are mutually exclusive, while ordinary cross-type memberships remain possible. Contest links prioritize actual faction pairs.
- The world board shows strain, challenges and histories. Leadership and institutional turning points appear in the Life Chronicle. Heirs inherit institutional history, never living officeholders, rosters or active crises.
- Splits and reunions apply to family, community and professional groups with adequate membership; rival households remain anchored to their existing family. Cross-kind mergers and direct permanent personal NPC rivalries are not introduced by this change.
