# Lifely 1.10.0 — Household Economy & Care

## New mechanics
1. Protected emergency savings ($500 or $1,500 transfers from real existing cash, and reversible withdrawals). The family reserve is included exactly once in net worth and is not counted as a new reward.
2. Child-specific educational savings ($500 or $1,500 per deposit). Education support spends $1,000 from that child's earmarked money once per year, improving actual child talent and resilience.
3. Four ongoing priorities: balanced household, saving expenses, investing in children's learning, or prioritizing care. Choices change annual costs and/or trust, stress and education. Annual consequences are visible.
4. Three care responsibility models with a partner: shared, solo, or outsourced. The last increases annual living expenses; other choices adjust stress and relationship consequences.
5. Optional agreed finances with a partner. Contributions are deducted from their actual independently simulated NPC savings and added to the household's annual net flow; they are NOT taxed as earnings or minted.
6. Sibling mediation changes existing sibling rivalry, cooperation and family bonds; once yearly per pair.
7. Succession divides the general estate separately from living children's restricted education money. A selected heir gets their earmark, siblings keep theirs, preferred-heir routing stays intact, and earlier family planning history remains visible.
8. UI: Money tab receives an actionable household board with financial summaries, child funds, partner savings, yearly outcomes and history. People tab shows the family plan. Old version-2 saves stay accepted, and household history is bounded.
