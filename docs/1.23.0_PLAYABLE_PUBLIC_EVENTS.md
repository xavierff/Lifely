# Lifely 1.23.0 — Playable Public Events (Build 33)

## What changed

Public gatherings are no longer just passive organization status updates. The player can **host a real scheduled public event** and play a three-round, choice-driven activity with animated visual gauges, portraits of actual NPC representatives, and consequences for the original organization network:

- **Interactive negotiations:** establish terms, handle concessions and publish a settlement. Fairness, pressure and group trust determine agreement, stalemate or escalation.
- **Community festivals:** program a shared event, respond to a setback and decide who gets recognition. Cooperation can produce a successful celebration; favoritism can divide the organizers.
- **Professional competitions:** set judging criteria, respond to a difficult challenge and present evidence. Outcomes include merit, unresolved ties and contested results, tied to real family/professional/rival organizations.
- **Leadership elections:** when a qualifying real organization has *at least two existing adult members*, the player can participate in three campaign stages. The incumbent and challenger are actual stored NPCs. The group chooses based on existing candidate trust, cohesion and the player's openly expressed endorsements; the player cannot create voters or a fake candidate. A successful election updates the group's actual leader, which affects subsequent intergroup leadership transitions.

Each stage has three unique authored options, including a strictly neutral choice. Existing groups' standing/project progress, the live intergroup relation and real NPC trust/rivalry respond to player input. Fair options can increase cooperation; coercive decisions can cause lasting public disputes. No money is created, no RNG rolls are added, and no income, career promotion or personal reputation is awarded for an autonomously occurring festival.

## Gameplay and responsiveness

- Starting one activity costs **one regular annual action**. The next three interactive rounds are free and can be completed even with zero annual actions remaining. Players can leave without an artificial penalty; unfinished events are automatically archived if their real public gathering expires or a participant becomes unavailable.
- A single original public-event ID can be played **once**. Future independently scheduled events can be hosted again without replay farming.
- The World tab displays all eligible scheduled events and format choices. Life, People and Work show the active challenge and its results; only current real events are offered. Animated React Native progress meters show Preparation, Fairness, Engagement and Pressure, plus the current chapter and prior measurable NPC effects.
- Significant completed events become entries in the existing Life Chronicle, contribute bounded earned social-influence evidence once when meaningful, and provide callbacks to future NPC narrative episodes.

## Legacy and save safety

- Older version-2 offline saves remain compatible because the new `playableEvents` field is optional and initialized when necessary.
- Ancestral public-event memories can be inherited, but not former contestants, votes, leadership or free assets. A new generation requires living people and a newly scheduled event to host another activity.
- Bounded state: one active event; at most 48 played original event IDs, 24 completed event records, 24 news items and 10 ancestral notes. Validation checks round ordering, candidate identities, bounded metrics, decision history and completion phases.

## Regression release gate

Tests cover: four actually eligible modes, stage progression and free decisions after paid entry; community celebration; merit versus controversy; elections with incumbent/new-leader/neutral outcomes; real NPC trust and resource conservation; reputation deduplication and Chronicle; voluntary departure and automatic interruption; unavailable candidates; leadership change propagated into later years; inherited institutions and save corruption; and browser 320/375/430px layouts. Merge only after both full engine and full browser/Expo suites pass for the final commit.

This version is **1.23.0**, iOS build **33**, Android versionCode **33**. No store submissions, signed native archives or scheduled automations are changed.
