# 1.57.0 — Dynamic Property Market & Real Estate Empires
Build 67; stacked on Build 66 with both corporate syntax corrections included.
- Separate, persistent marketplace of 30 independent properties, across apartments, homes, duplexes, offices, hotels and shopping centers. NPC purchases use actual savings; NPC ownership and tenancy IDs persist.
- Market price index, demand, booms, cooling and crashes; values change annually.
- Cash or 30% down mortgage acquisitions; negotiated seller quotes, 20-year amortization, phase-dependent interest, principal repayment, refinancing and two-missed-payment foreclosure.
- Real NPC tenants; rent flows from tenant cash to landlord, with vacancy, condition, maintenance, rent changes and tenant selection.
- Renovation and two-year property development improve condition and value; player portfolio has separate annual rent/maintenance/interest/principal figures.
- Mortgage balances reduce net worth; old legacy property inventory remains separate. On succession, positive property equity already included in estate cash may be retained by exchanging that cash for the same property, preserving mortgage and avoiding double-counting. If allocated cash is insufficient, the property returns to market. A playable heir's own NPC property transfers directly.
- Market is simplified: no individual unit management, geographic parcels, mortgage credit underwriting or separately incorporated real estate investment companies; commercial tenants are individual NPCs. Annual cash accounting is separate from legacy income/tax summary. Construction projects improve existing assets, not new buildings on raw land.
