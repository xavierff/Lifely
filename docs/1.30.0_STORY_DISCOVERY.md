# Lifely 1.30.0 — Story Discovery & Purposeful Lives

The Life screen groups enabled optional decisions into expandable living-world story cards. All available responses are shown together, sorted by current story focus; genuine known resolution ages are shown without inventing a deadline. Public delivery and accountability outcomes now appear in the Life Chronicle. Old saves remain compatible; no new persistent state schema. Release 1.30.0 build 40.
