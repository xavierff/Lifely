# Lifely 1.19.0 — Competing Futures (Build 29)

## New playable system
Existing NPCs can now intersect with the player's life in three grounded ways:

- **Career rivalry:** Only a living NPC with a current career ambition and an actual matching occupation triggers an overlap. Players can share expertise, compete openly or maintain professional boundaries. Decisions adjust the existing character's trust/rivalry, the player's real performance, and the NPC's ongoing goal progress.
- **Business rivalry and partnership:** An existing business plus a relevant former-founder or family friend's ambition can initiate collaboration or market competition. Choices affect the *existing* company's demand and the NPC's actual social profile, never a fake new venture or invented sales balance.
- **Family conflicts and commitments:** Real adult relatives can ask for support or boundaries when their own financial, connection or reconciliation goals overlap. Financial assistance transfers $200 from the player's cash to the living NPC's cash, with strict affordability and conservation; neutral choices do not change bonds, stats, or wealth.

Overlapping circumstances generate one optional collision at a time (maximum two open). A second chapter can return after two years, using the actual current job, company, NPC relationship and goal; relationships can heal, turn into rivalry, diverge or remain unresolved. Ignored events expire without stat penalties or interruptions to aging. No new random rolls.

## World and save continuity
- The same existing NPC IDs, goal IDs, profession IDs, business sector IDs and relationship roles are used. When a career changes or a company is sold, stale business/job effects are not applied, and the ending reflects the divergent paths.
- Actual decisions are captured as player/NPC stats, business demand and cash deltas. The People tab shows the full two-character confrontation, accessible choice controls, measured effects and completed stories. Life previews ready situations; Work previews career/business conflicts.
- Important outcomes are added to the Life Chronicle and referenced in the original Character Director. Significant family/professional tensions persist as **ancestral summaries**, without resurrecting previous-generation NPCs.
- Saves retain version 2 compatibility. The optional competingFutures state caps active events at 2, completed at 24, news at 25, deduplication at 45 and ancestral summaries at 10, with strict validation and a per-person cooldown.

## Release quality gate
Full npm test, Expo dependency verification, all-platform Expo export and Playwright E2E are mandatory on final commit. New tests cover career skill/trust differences, business demand, financial conservation, unaffordable contributions, no-op choices, delayed callbacks, changing jobs, sold companies, missing NPCs, legacy summaries, malformed saves, and responsive 320/375/430px widths.

No TestFlight or Play uploads and no automation changes.
