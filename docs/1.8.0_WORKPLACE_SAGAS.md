# Lifely 1.8.0 — Workplace Sagas

## Feature
Twelve fully original, industry-specific professional stories support all 150 ordinary careers. Each has three authored chapters over five in-game years with three decisions per chapter. Two named coworkers form persistent relationships and develop independently of player choices. Players can seek collaboration, demonstrate technical craft, or compete ambitiously, generating distinct effects on trust, rivalry, professional standing, performance, stress and annual review prospects.
Resignation, a career switch or dismissal interrupts a story; abandoned chapters do not magically produce success. All follow-ups are voluntary and reminders age out. The current-life journal and inherited career records are bounded.
A separate repair fixes the Personal Ambition technical-skill milestone so it can be completed.

## Automated quality gates
npm test — original 150-career coverage, deterministic chapters, all actions, independent NPC progression, callbacks, pay and promotion effect, job changes, missed deadlines, legacy and skill fix.
npx expo install --check — supported dependencies.
npx expo export --platform all — multi-platform static bundling.
npm run test:e2e — saved story navigation, age callbacks, and compact 320/375/430-pixel browser layout.

## Remaining signoffs
- Real native iOS and Android device testing: restored old saves, large text, accessibility announcements and touch input.
- Extended salary and professional-standing balance review to ensure paths have a tradeoff without an exploit.
- Independent player comparison against BitLife across session enjoyment, NPC attachment, voluntary replay, game pace and crash-free operation.
- No signed native release, in-app purchases, subscriptions or app-store submission without separate user approval.
