# Lifely 1.31.0 — Playful Life Expansion

Thirteen original age-specific outings, each with three authored chapters and three strategic choices. Childhood activities introduce the same recurring classmates used by school showcases; teen crush scenes require an existing age-appropriate peer and never force a relationship; adult dates require a living adult partner. Connection, momentum, tension and skills drive five endings, with one ordinary action per outing per year. Existing contacts receive bond/trust effects and scheduled follow-ups; no automatic money, careers or relationship creation. Rare memories are a bounded unique collection and can be inherited as historical records only. Four new competitive hobby circuits extend the eight existing ones to twelve (table tennis, theater/improv, puzzle rally and craft design). Life-screen discovery, school social summaries, animated outcome feedback respecting reduced motion and the existing mute control, and Chronicle entries. Existing scene and hobby save structures remain compatible. Eight engine regressions, two mobile/browser tests and revised hobby-count check. Native device sound and performance still require device testing. Version 1.31.0 build 41. Merge only after prerequisite PR #31 and full CI.
