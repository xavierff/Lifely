# 1.70.0 — Advanced Sports Gameplay
Build 80. Adds stateful twelve-turn football and basketball matches. Passes can retain possession and build attacking quality; shots end possession; defensive decisions determine interceptions and opponent scoring. Opponent team strength, changing tactical shape, open/threatened lanes, current form, fatigue and stance alter the odds. Once per match, change stance or bring on a fresh teammate. Football scores goals; basketball scores two or three points. Four fictional teams in each sport, three fixtures per season, six total fixtures including simulated opponent-only matches, sorted standings, history, persistent statistics and championship legacy records. Existing negotiated team IDs determine the team where applicable; unaffiliated athletes use their sport's default team. One activity per match, a forfeit records a loss, no extra cash reward, completed results feed actual sports wins/losses/form and adult promotion eligibility. Active matches block aging and resume from saves. Eleven engine tests and one narrow browser regression authored. This is a compact turn-based match, not a full real-time 90-minute simulation, full roster management, or independently scheduled multi-division league. Prior arcade and sporting competitions remain available. CI pending; no signed build or physical-device QA.
