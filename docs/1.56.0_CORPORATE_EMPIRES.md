# 1.56.0 — Corporate Empires & Interactive Business Management
Build 66; stacked on Build 65.

- Expandable CEO dashboard for acquired firms; launch products, market them, expand regional reach, reinvest personal capital, grow staff capacity, recruit qualified NPCs, promote/dismiss staff and appoint executives.
- Seller negotiation with discounted opening bids, counteroffers and time limits; listed-firm premium control bid requires a minority holding. Not a hostile shareholder vote or competing auction.
- Listing, 10% minority share trades, public float, shareholder dividends and proportional player equity. Public market is abstract rather than a separately simulated exchange/order book.
- Corporate loans, 7% annual interest, repayment and simplified distressed restructuring; debt reduces equity.
- Group acquired businesses as subsidiaries without duplicate value; annual operating profit reflects new products, geographic reach and management.
- Existing acquired company assets remain part of player net worth; previous player estate still liquidates their controlled firms and minority shares into existing estate accounting. Heirs' own NPC firms retain ownership.
- Annual, bounded simulation; five regional reach levels rather than individual countries or offices. Product levels rather than individually named products. No dedicated billionaire leaderboard or playable inherited corporate ownership beyond the existing succession rules.
- Ten engine tests plus one mobile browser regression. Verification required before merge.
