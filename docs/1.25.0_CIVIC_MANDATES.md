# Lifely 1.25.0 — Living Civic Mandates and Multi-Year Organization Campaigns

## Beyond personal rivalries: collective decisions with consequences

Lifely 1.25.0 adds a second linked layer after 1.24.0's real NPC relationship aftershocks. Existing **family councils**, **professional guilds**, **community assemblies**, and **rival households** can now launch genuine **eight-year civic and professional mandates** rooted in their recorded public events and ongoing historical arcs.

Each initiative has its own topic based on the existing organizations:
- Family care and neighborhood support
- Apprenticeships and fair access to technical skills
- Shared community spaces disputed by rival households
- Community program standards and shared commitments

Organizations pursue their own positions every two years using their actual stored cohesion, standing, tension, previous public-event records, and real leaders' relationships. Campaigns track **support for both groups, common ground, evidence prepared, and resistance**. Those are transparent organization metrics, **not fabricated voter counts or polls**.

## Playable consequences over several years

After the first two years, players may choose to build evidence and negotiate cooperation, advocate for whichever group currently has less support, or stay independent. Every meaningful intervention changes the real current NPC leaders, both source groups, actual intergroup trust/competition, and a relevant player skill. The independent option is strictly neutral apart from the normal annual activity cost.

Campaigns can conclude in **joint adoption, a priority victory for one group, partial compromise, gridlock, or dissolution**. Projects and reputations can benefit or suffer on the actual organizations. Passive organization progress does not generate player cash, promotions, fabricated assets, or earned social standing. Abandoned invitations expire without penalizing the player.

Only one public mandate can run at a time, with lengthy cooldowns. This supplements the existing playable public events and long-lived personal rivalries rather than replacing them.

## World integration

- Full World board with the actual two leaders, group-support gauges, evidence/resistance meters, proposal timeline and every recorded player response.
- Life and People show contextual compact decision cards; Work shows skills-access proposals. Board remains visible in the year the player makes a choice.
- Major resolutions enter the **Life Chronicle** and influence later NPC story context. Earned Social Influence is counted once for each substantive player decision.
- Leadership changes use actual current members and are documented. Missing real organizations cause proposals to pause and then dissolve; deceased NPCs are never recreated.
- Old version-2 saves remain compatible. Historical policy outcomes survive descendants only as memories, never as inherited offices, permissions, wages or automatically loyal acquaintances.

## Storage and QA

Strict state limits: one active campaign, 24 archived outcomes, 36 grounded source IDs, 12 milestones per campaign, at most 4 decisions, 25 news records and 10 ancestral notes. Deterministic annual updates introduce no extra RNG or server dependencies.

New core regression and browser tests cover genuine organization formation, group-specific issues, two-year progression, both types of intervention, neutral choices, autonomous resolutions and no free rewards, leadership changes and loss, public memory and legacy handoff, old saves, corruption, and 320/375/430px responsive layouts.

Release target: **Lifely 1.25.0, iOS build 35, Android versionCode 35**. Merge requires both full engine and full browser/Expo checks to pass on the final PR revision. No signed builds, store submissions, or automations.
