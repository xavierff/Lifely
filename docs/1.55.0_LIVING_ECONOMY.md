# 1.55.0 — Living Economy & Independent Businesses
Build 65. Build 64 passed CI and was merged.

- Existing NPCs invest their own savings to found bounded firms across five sectors. Real cash, capital, demand, reputation, payroll, annual profit, expansion, payouts and insolvency.
- Companies hire existing qualified unemployed NPCs; layoffs and company closure update job records. Existing NPC career simulation provides promotions. Player applications consume an activity and establish a real company contract; annual gross pay is reflected in company payroll and player income.
- Buy and sell full companies at book value, with funds exchanged with actual NPC owners. Acquired companies remain autonomous and contribute to player net worth. Commercial partnerships improve existing player-business and NPC-company demand; competition affects both.
- Firms transfer to adult NPC heirs where available, or to an eligible NPC buyer. A playable heir who already owns a company retains it. Old player-owned companies are liquidated into the existing player estate accounting rather than duplicated.
- Work and Money dashboards list owners, employees, vacancies, balances, demand and news. World clock and company records persist across succession.
- Limits: 20 companies and six NPC staff per firm; annual stylized economics with external demand and generic labor markets. Family enterprises from 1.54 remain distinct. Applications and acquisitions are immediate if eligible; no hostile takeover battles, bidding, interviews, or direct operational management of acquired firms yet. Insolvent firms use simplified closure, and wages accrue for the simulated year.
- Nine core tests and one narrow browser regression added; verification pending.
