# Lifely 1.11.0 — Family Transitions

## Scope
This update extends existing NPC family and household systems, rather than creating an independent cash balance or replacing the previous family stories. It is original fiction, not legal, care, educational or financial advice.

### Separation and co-parenting
Ending a relationship records the original ex-partner and former-marriage status. Existing shared financial contributions are disabled. When living minor children exist, players can update shared, resident or other-household co-parenting plans. The annual ledger accounts for financial support received and given, debiting the actual adult character whose resources fund it. Family bonds, trust, cooperation, stress and children's wellbeing respond to decisions.

### Property
An owned property can enter an optional fictional dispute with an ex-partner. Regular sale is locked until the matter is closed. Resolution choices are:
- Equal split: sell at 95% market value, divide proceeds 50/50.
- Negotiated compromise: sell at 95%, deduct a $300 simulated mediation fee, then divide the remainder 70/30.
- Buyout: keep the property and pay 40% market value to the ex-partner from actual player cash.
- Withdraw: keep the current property and the factual history of unresolved disagreement.
If the partner or asset becomes unavailable during the annual update, the dispute is closed safely.

### Elders and young adults
Care plans for living parents and grandparents aged 60+ can prioritize independence, coordinated family care or direct support. The latter options increase annual family expenses and improve some relationship outcomes. A personal check-in is limited to once per year.

From ages 14–24, the player can talk to a child about goals, learning or work. At 18, the child chooses its own direction from its persistent personality; at 21, it follows through. These choices change NPC education, cash, debt and learning potential; they do not require the player to give up autonomy, nor bill the player for the child's self-directed tuition.

### Quality gates

- npx expo install --check: dependencies.
- npx expo export --platform all: static iOS/Android/web export.
- npm run test:e2e: People/Money navigation, co-parenting choices, property transfer, youth guidance and narrow-screen layout.

### Remaining limitations
The family legal, property and care models are simplified fictional systems, not representations of real laws. External player comparisons, multi-generation manual playtests, native screen reader / dynamic-type testing, real device save restoration and balance review remain required. No signed build, TestFlight, Play Store submission or automation changes were performed as part of this feature.
