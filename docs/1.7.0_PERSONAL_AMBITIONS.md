# Lifely 1.7.0 — Personal Ambitions: Verification & Quality Benchmark

## User-visible behavior
- Sixteen **original**, optional goals turn a loosely directed life into a set of player-selected multi-year arcs.
- Each goal lists three concrete achievements and their current actual values. Start counters and wealth baselines are captured at the moment of commitment. Earlier actions do not create retroactive credit.
- Optional turn decisions become available after two simulated years, once per arc: sustainable pace (−5 stress), bold investment (−$1,000, +3 stress, larger prize), or ask a companion (+8 bond and +3 kindness).
- Completing all milestones grants a small simulated prize, stat effects and a saved chronicle entry. Expiry after six years and voluntary abandonment are distinct archived outcomes. Neither blocks aging.
- Real existing hobbies, social conversations, character drama, business actions and financial activity count. No extra grind button was added.
- Across generations, a child's save inherits **only completed ancestral ambition history**; an ancestor's ongoing campaign cannot become the heir's obligation.
- Age and role gates: some missions require a real job or business; older saved lives are supported as missing optional data, and all persisted arrays have bounds.

## Physical-device and editorial signoff still required
1. Start at age 10 and at age 40; choose a goal and confirm every screen is clear on a compact iPhone, tablet, and mid-range Android device, in native builds.
2. Confirm no double rewards when restoring the save on a milestone boundary, after a career switch, after a financial year, or when dying on a deadline.
3. Verify social and financial campaigns cannot be exploited by entering and leaving the same screen without taking the specified action.
4. Confirm a companion relationship changes as promised and survives saving; verify the debt/cash account and simulated reward ledger remain consistent.
5. Complete a full six-year campaign and abandon another. Continue as a child and confirm only completed stories reach their heir.
6. Review all 16 story blurbs and achievement wording for coherence and accuracy.
7. Check accessibility: each milestone has a labelled progress indicator; buttons remain 48px high and responsive for 320–430px; settings should honor large text and touch assistance.

## Better-than-BitLife rubric — evidence required, not a claim

| Criterion | What to measure in independent playtests | Desired threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement | Median voluntary play time (new players) | Greater than BitLife under matched sessions |
| Replay value | % of players voluntarily choosing another life after completing one | Greater than comparator |
| Meaningful decisions | Player-rated perceived consequence (1–5), plus recorded replay branch variation | Better mean and narrower worst-case |
| Pace | Time between meaningful choices, and number of repeated prompts | Fewer repetitive interruptions at equivalent game depth |
| Character attachment | Player recall of NPC names and impact one session later | Better recall/attachment |
| UX and accessibility | Task completion, crash-free rate, compact screens and screen-reader checks | At least comparator parity, no high-priority blockers |
| Breadth | Independently sampled feature coverage and content quality (not just scenario count) | Parity in core requested life paths, strength in differentiators |
| Retention | D1 and D7 return among consenting playtest participants | Greater than comparator after similar onboarding |

No measured values exist yet. Recruiting playtesters, running device builds, and making a public competitive claim require a separate decision. Lifely remains a development candidate; no TestFlight or Google Play submission was performed.
