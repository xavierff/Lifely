# Lifely 1.17.0 — Autonomous Social Crossroads (Build 27)

This release deepens the living world by reading real relationships generated by the autonomous alumni and social-network engines.

## What changes
- NPCs continue forming alliances, friendships, rivalries, estrangements, partnerships and trust breaches without direct player intervention.
- Real link changes can trigger an optional **Social Crossroads** invitation, with the exact two NPCs, their current relationship and their real trust/rivalry scores.
- Players can mediate, back one person or respect independence. Decisions directly mutate the actual link, each NPC's trust/rivalry, and relevant player stats. Choices record actual clamped deltas, not projected rewards.
- Two years later a followup may become available. NPCs evolve independently; the eventual outcome uses their then-current relationship rather than scripted promises.
- Outcomes include alliance, reconciliation, rivalry, estrangement, unresolved tension, observed decisions, interruption and unanswered opportunities.
- The Life tab previews playable opportunities. The People tab includes two-character scenes, meters, accessible choice controls, actual impact pills and completed story records.
- Major outcomes appear on the Life Chronicle. Character Director episodes reference the wider social history. Selected outcomes become ancestral recaps.

## Design constraints
- Optional v2 save data: maximum 3 active invitations, 24 completed records, 24 news updates, 48 deduplication records and 8 ancestral summaries, all validated.
- Earlier saves remain compatible. Missing/deceased characters and expired interactions are never resurrected.
- Only existing NPCs and relationships can create invitations; repeat invitations are throttled.
- No additional RNG, money or paid systems. Annual aging remains unblocked. Each choice uses one ordinary yearly activity; ignored opportunities expire without stat penalties.
- Established romantic partnerships that break have partner links cleared; new NPC partnerships initiated through player mediation are professional unless independently formed romantically.

## Verification
Full npm test, Expo dependency compatibility, all-platform Expo export, and Playwright browser tests on the final feature commit. Tests cover saves, real graph changes, neutral decisions, callback ages, softlocks, RNG/cash neutrality, interruption, bounds, ancestry, mobile widths and reload persistence. No build upload or automation changes.
