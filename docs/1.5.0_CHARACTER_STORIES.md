# Lifely 1.5.0 (build 15) — Character-Driven Stories QA

## New playable systems
- Original Life Director: 8 distinct conflicts, each with 3 authored phases and 3 responses in every phase.
- Episodes draw actual active-life family members, adult classmates, friends and professional contacts.
- Episodes are weighted by player age, career, money pressures and relationships; recently used themes are less likely to recur.
- Story decisions are influenced by player's relevant skills, personality-appropriate action, NPC loyalty and shifting emotional stakes. Results are deterministic for a given RNG seed.
- Player pays one annual activity to start, then may choose 3 stages without further activities or advance-year. Walking away remains possible.
- Permanent impact on actual social bonds and world profiles, happiness, stress, professional progress, business demand or reputation. A delayed echo fires two years later without requiring an interaction.
- Saved local story history and heir inheritance; additional achievements for story progression.
- Optional invitations expire; they never obstruct the main year advancement or other life features.

CI checks gameplay, old saves, deterministic choices, accessibility and 320/375/430px browser layouts. Report results from the actual final commit.

## Device and editorial gates
1. On physical iPhone and Android hardware, start a story after an age-up, confirm tap targets and progress bars remain visible with large fonts.
2. Verify all original sound effects play according to mute and silent-mode settings; Reduced Motion disables spring animations.
3. Review every story for coherent character motivations and genuinely distinct responses (including successful bold approaches that have social costs).
4. Save after each round, force close, restore and finish; check that income, relationships and stats are never double-applied.
5. Finish a difficult story, age two years and verify the echo applies exactly once. Ignore the invitation in another life and ensure it closes without blocking the year button.
6. Test family, school, adult professions, bankruptcy, justice and life ending; check that old stored saves validate.
7. Continue as a child. Confirm completed stories become family history while active obligations do not follow the heir.
8. Human playtests should measure coherence, decision diversity, replay rate and time-to-boredom against BitLife; do not claim market superiority without evidence.

This is a development build, not a TestFlight submission or publicly released build. All narrative characters and events are fictional.
