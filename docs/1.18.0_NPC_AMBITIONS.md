# Lifely 1.18.0 — NPC Ambitions and Decades of Independent Lives (Build 28)

## Player-facing gameplay
- Named family members, friends and former classmates begin **their own** long-term ambitions rooted in their existing personalities, careers and motivations. Seven distinct plans cover career growth, financial security, close relationships, original creative work, community service, life changes, and reconciliation.
- Goals develop autonomously every in-game year, independently of the player's actions. They record progress, pressure, personal setbacks and eventual achievements or complicated endings.
- Real setbacks can change priorities: existing financial debt can drive an NPC to seek security; serious rivalry and lost trust can redirect them toward reconciliation; aging changes career ambitions into family and community priorities.
- Two **optional** conversations are possible over several years. The player can encourage, question or respect independence. Responses affect actual NPC trust, rivalry and the protagonist's relevant stats; the NPC may succeed without any intervention.
- Achieved goals produce changes in the original character's world, not cosmetic only: a promotion in an existing job, project completion, lower debt using their own money, stronger real relationships, civic service, a change of alumni residence or a reduction in an existing rivalry. Never create phantom assets, spouses or children.
- The Life tab previews someone needing a response; the People tab has portraits, progress meters, pressure, decision tradeoffs, pivots and durable outcomes.
- Important goals appear in the Life Chronicle and influence context in the existing Character Director; selected successes and redirections leave concise records for the next generation.

## Compatibility and safety
- Version 2 offline save schema remains unchanged, with optional bounded npcAmbitions state. Existing saved lives without it remain valid.
- Maximum 10 active goals, 32 completed, 30 news, 10 ancestral summaries, one active goal per NPC and a four-year cooldown before an NPC pursues another.
- At most two goals begin each year; invitations and followups are optional, and no scene blocks aging. Using one regular activity pays for a response, with no extra action cost. Neither autonomous growth nor story updates spend extra RNG or create money.
- Missing/deceased characters end as interrupted without being revived. No new fictional household children, made-up financial balances, or external accounts.

## QA gate
Full npm test and the Expo compatibility, iOS/Android/web export and Playwright checks must pass on the final feature commit before merging. New regression coverage includes goal catalog, independent progress, real career/financial effects, response costs and outcomes, pivots after debt and damaged relationships, no-random/no-money side effects, optional and missed followups, inherited recaps, old saves, save validation and 320–430px screens.

No store submission or automation changes.
