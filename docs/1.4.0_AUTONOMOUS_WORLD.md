# Lifely 1.4.0 (build 14): Autonomous living world QA

## New systems
- A four-person school cohort with persistent distinct classmate IDs, trust, rivalry, preferences, and school-project memory.
- At adulthood (age 18), participating classmates become fully persistent alumni who can be consulted using the existing personal-conversation mechanics.
- Each new year advances friendships, partnerships (professional or fictional adult romantic), rivalry, career promotions, relocations and occasional family milestones without requiring the player to push a button.
- Fictional NPC stories in six categories: reunion, creative project, local enterprise, community project, rivalry, and mentorship.
- The world creates chapter invitations at +1, +3 and +5 years after the original turning point. Every chapter presents three original choices; passing on them never blocks life progression. Decisions can affect existing job performance, business demand, friendships, fame, confidence, kindness and reputation.
- The social world displays a roster, relationship history, opportunity timeline and archive of completed stories.
- Data is stored locally with the existing save version 2. Old 1.3.0 saves remain valid without migration files.

## Automated verification

```sh
npm test
npx expo install --check
npx expo export --platform all
npm run test:e2e
```
The Actions workflow runs these checks. Only claim success after the final commit is green.

## Manual QA remaining
1. Replay school projects across ages 6–18. Verify all four classmates are distinct and carry accurate trust and rivalry into adulthood at age 18.
2. Advance twenty years without interacting with social contacts; the autonomous world should evolve independently, with a mix of positive, negative and complicated relationship transitions.
3. Check that partnerships only occur among simulated adults and that unrelated NPCs do not suddenly become the player’s partner without a deliberate player choice.
4. Accept, support, ignore and decline a major opportunity at the correct age. Confirm stages are +1, +3 and +5, outcomes are replay safe, and no one becomes locked out of advancing life.
5. Test the new contacts in both their special alumni actions and the existing three-round conversations.
6. Confirm business, entertainment, politics and sports status are updated where the story context supports it; no simulated rewards should create double-counted money.
7. Restore old saves and all three game slots; check deep into life and after continuing as a living child. Ancestor-only promises should not carry over, but historical archives should.
8. Verify 320 px and larger phones, text scaling, screen readers, OS reduced motion and original SFX on real iOS/Android hardware.
9. Run unbiased player studies evaluating unpredictability, narrative coherence and replay value versus BitLife; superiority should not be claimed from feature totals alone.

## Fictional content
All characters, romances, civic institutions, career milestones, financial outcomes and opportunities are fictional. Romantic partner status between NPCs is not an instruction or statement about real individuals. This build is not a signed App Store or Play Store release.
