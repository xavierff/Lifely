# 1.60.0 — Living Neighborhoods, Travel & Everyday Life
Build 70. Extends the international city system. 54 fictional districts (three per city), three housing cost tiers, district visits, recurring actual NPC residents, cultural progress, three community story templates with three decisions and delayed outcomes, three travel themes with guided/independent branches, study certificates and retirement communities. Interactive district selector in World, not a literal street map. Housing costs modify existing annual baseline rather than adding a second rent charge. Existing property ownership blocks neighborhood moves when the player owns a legacy home. Local property/company inventories are still shared markets, not separate city-specific markets. No real-time calendar or full city-specific school/career transfer system. Ten engine tests plus one browser regression added; CI required.
