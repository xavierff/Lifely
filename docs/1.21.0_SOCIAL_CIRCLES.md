# Lifely 1.21.0 — Living Social Circles (Build 31)

## The living world organizes itself
- People form **family councils**, **career networks and enterprise guilds**, **community assemblies**, and **rival households**. Each institution references existing named adults in the simulated world. Rival households only appear when an actually known alumnus has children and a significant recorded rivalry. No fictional human members or fabricated family relationships are added.
- Organizations track leadership, identified real members, standing, cohesion, tension, active projects and historical outcomes. They grow through autonomous, deterministic (no additional random rolls) multi-year projects that can succeed, stall and restart. Relationships between actual alumni contribute to their internal dynamics.
- Group membership is updated from the original NPC records: when characters die or become unavailable, leadership changes or the organization becomes dormant. No missing character is revived or silently replaced by an unrelated rival household. An old organization can be renewed by living NPCs in later generations.
- Groups form gradually, one per in-game year, with four distinct category slots. Invitations are bounded to one open group decision at a time, with four-year spacing. They never block yearly aging.

## Player decisions with lasting consequences
- The player can support an existing organization's project, challenge its priorities or respect its independent trajectory. The first two options change the actual NPC's trust/rivalry, the player's relevant stats and real organization standing, tension and project progress. The neutral response costs a normal activity but changes none of these values.
- Two-years-later callbacks resolve as alliance, negotiated compromise, rivalry, independence, interruption, dissolution or an unresolved ending based on the group's actual circumstances. Unanswered invitations expire without penalties.
- Significant outcomes also enter the **Life Chronicle**, inform **Story Director** context and change the appropriate earned **Social Influence** category only when the player participated in a meaningful decision. Autonomous group project success itself grants no player reputation, income or other reward.

## Multiple generations
- Group identity, project outcomes, historical archives and organizational standing persist into the heir's life. After inheritance organizations are **dormant** and have no automatically living members, leader, partner, salary, inheritance payment or player allegiance.
- Group membership resumes only when eligible, real adult NPCs exist in the next generation. A rival household remains attached to its original, actual representative rather than being assigned an unrelated rival. Ancestral summaries reflect the old generation's relationship to each group.

## Game feel and UX
- New cards in World show organizations, representative portraits, real members, relationship meters, long-term initiatives and historical milestones.
- Life, People and Work show contextual compact group invitations with three real choices and measurable trust, rivalry, standing and progress outcomes. Completed stories and inherited organizational memories are browsable.
- One open invitation and four years between group invitations avoid overwhelming the player with prompts.

## QA, storage, and safety
- The offline save remains schema version 2; old saves without socialCircles stay valid.
- Bounds: maximum four organizations, one active invitation, 28 historical invitations, eight events per group, 24 news items and ten ancestral summaries. Strict identity and state guards prevent corrupted membership and impossible histories.
- Added engine tests cover grounded group founding by type, rival-household validity, independent projects, responses and tradeoffs, neutral activity consumption, expired/ceased NPC cases, the two-year callback and Life Chronicle, earned social influence deduplication, generational dormancy and renewal, gradual formation, save validation and normal age-up.
- Added browser E2E checks for family, career, rival household, multiyear stories, intergenerational memory, save/reload, app tab layout and 320/375/430px widths.
- Require the full core regression suite, Expo compatibility, all-platform export and Playwright browser checks on the final PR revision **before merging**.

No automations, store submissions, signed archives or public releases are performed.
