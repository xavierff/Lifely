# Lifely 1.34.0 — Family Legacy and Inheritance Choices

Optional equal or 60/40 preferred-child estate plans use actual living children and allocate the unearmarked estate exactly. The original inheritance behavior is preserved if no written plan exists. Dedicated child education funds remain separate. Existing adult sibling rivalry can generate a two-year disagreement with mediation, revision, explanation or delay. Actual siblings receive their allocated funds on generational continuation; unresolved disputes affect the challenger's existing trust and rivalry. Historical plans are archived rather than inherited as active commitments. The People screen shows estimated shares and disputes, and the Chronicle records major decisions. Fictional simulation only, not legal guidance. Five engine regressions and one mobile browser persistence test. Build 44. Merge after 1.33 and full CI.
