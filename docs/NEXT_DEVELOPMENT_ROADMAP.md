# Lifely next development roadmap — bundled milestones
Prioritize stability: verify and merge pending changes in order. Use regression tests, save compatibility and mobile bundle checks before any TestFlight build. Avoid version churn by grouping related systems in each milestone.
## 1.60 — Living neighborhoods, travel & daily stories
Interactive neighborhood map, resident contacts, venues, festivals, trips with branching itineraries, cultural progression, local housing and employment, city-specific NPC relationships, long-distance family events, international education and retirement.
## 1.61 — Relationships, households & generations
NPC matchmaking and weddings, autonomous households, sibling rivalry, family gatherings and conflicts, parenting choices, household budgets, divorce/custody, intergenerational businesses and property inheritance, genealogy and family goals.
## 1.62 — Entertainment, sports & specialist careers
Playable sports seasons, scouting, teams, tournaments, film productions, casting, music releases, touring, influencer analytics, courtroom cases, clinical cases, election debates, rivals, agents and year-spanning career arcs.
## 1.63 — World simulation & connected economy
Country-specific firms, property markets and economic cycles, actual corporate boards, competitive offers, venture deals, NPC portfolios, succession of operating businesses, regional shocks and geopolitical effects, consistent unified annual ledger.
## 1.64 — Presentation, replayability & release readiness
Visual interaction, responsive navigation, animation and audio, accessible UI, onboarding, meaningful goals, scenario diversity, achievements/challenges, performance profiling, offline save recovery, migration validation, deterministic long-run stress testing, device QA and release audit.
Compare against user experience and measurable regression evidence before claiming superiority to other life simulators.
