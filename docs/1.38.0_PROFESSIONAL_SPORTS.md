# Lifely 1.38.0 — Professional Sports Contracts

Eight fictional football, basketball, tennis and track organizations; rank, wins, form and injury gates. Negotiated terms, salary counteroffers, early release fees, transfers, team training, two weak-season release condition and expiry. Negotiated salary replaces the generic world sports contract payment and remains part of world income. Existing generic contracts are unchanged. Tennis/track organizations represent training squads rather than real franchise transfers. Retirement ends active negotiations without transferring a contract to an heir. Work dashboard, Chronicle and inherited records. Five engine tests and one narrow mobile browser test. Build 48. Stacked on 1.37. No signed native build. Merge after prerequisites and CI.
