# Lifely 1.26.0 — The Real Implementation of Public Promises

## An additional six-year living-world campaign after each civic mandate

Lifely now distinguishes between agreeing on a policy and **actually delivering it**. A real completed 1.25 civic mandate can generate an independent six-year implementation project tied to the *same two living organizations*, its authentic source decision, original intergroup relationship, and current real representatives.

The delivery system measures **actual organizational progress, quality, public trust, and opposition**, not imaginary election polling, voters, government accounts or anonymous procurement bidders. Whether a policy is jointly adopted, one organization dominates the proposal, or the debate deadlocks affects the project's starting conditions. Over three distinct two-year chapters, the real groups' projects and relationships determine whether the program is **delivered, revised, stalled, or abandoned**. If participating members are no longer available, delivery pauses and may close without inventing a replacement.

## Decisions that affect implementation

An eligible player can:
1. Audit real delivery with both organizations, improving evidence quality, trust, and real NPC relationships.
2. Prioritize the first organization, improving its status at the expense of the second organization and increasing opposition.
3. Contribute professional skills if the player **actually holds a job or owns a business**. The project records the participant as a volunteer or real business contact. No invented income, accepted procurement contract, equity, royalty, fictitious budget or promotion.
4. Stay independent, changing no actual player stats, money, real NPC traits, original groups or network state beyond spending one normal activity.

Offers appear at appropriate implementation milestones, never block age progression, and expire neutrally. Passive outcomes adjust only original organization standing/projects and intergroup trust or competition—not player finances or personal reputation.

## Player-facing features

A new **Public Implementation** section in World provides actual NPC representatives, four transparent meters, project milestones, decision outcomes, named optional contacts, and completed results. Life and People show context previews; Work shows only professional skills-access projects. Current-year decisions remain visible instead of vanishing instantly.

The Life Chronicle remembers whether a specific public promise was delivered or stalled. Original NPCs may remember it in Story Director. Earned Social Influence only changes when the player made a substantive implementation decision; passive results earn nothing. An heir inherits historical policy memories, never living contacts or municipal power.
