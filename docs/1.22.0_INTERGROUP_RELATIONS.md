# Lifely 1.22.0 — Intergroup Relations, Public Events, and Changing Leaders (Build 32)

## Organizations now influence one another
Previously individual family councils, community assemblies, guilds and rival households could evolve independently. Lifely 1.22.0 connects actual, living organizations into a persistent intergroup world:

- Each distinct pair of eligible groups can establish a persistent relationship, tracking **mutual trust**, **competition**, statuses (allied, cooperating, competing, strained, neutral, dormant), **leaders**, actual shared members, years of contact, and an event-by-event chronicle.
- Relationships evolve each year from existing groups' cohesion, tension, shared membership, and rivalry type—not extra random rolls. Group members and institutions are always sourced from the existing NPC and social-circle records; relationships never manufacture new people, careers, money, or other assets.
- **Autonomous public gatherings:** pairs organize open skills fairs, community forums, cross-group assemblies or public dialogues between rival households. Initiatives progress annually and can succeed, become contested, pause or resume. Only the *real groups'* standing and long-term intergroup trust change automatically. Passive observation does not increase the player's reputation, cash, social connections or career.
- **Real leadership turnover:** when a group appoints a different living leader, the intergroup relationship records the transition and adjusts its trust/competition. A story follows the *current real representatives*; past leaders are never revived. If either group goes dormant, its public initiative pauses and obsolete invitations dissolve.

## The player can change multiple futures at once
- Optional intergroup invitations allow the player to broker a public pact, favor the first organization at the cost of the second, or leave both to act independently.
- Two years later, the representatives can return and the player can renew the pact, change sides or decline to interfere. Each meaningful response affects two actual organizations' standing/project progress, the real leaders' trust/rivalry, the genuine relationship between groups and a relevant player stat. Neutral replies consume a normal activity but change no group resources, money, NPC traits, reputation or RNG state.
- Outcome paths include **coalition, negotiated cooperation, public feud, fractured relations, independent futures, unanswered requests or dissolution**. Invitations expire and age-up never softlocks.
- Significant intergroup outcomes are archived in both groups' histories, the Life Chronicle, and later Story Director context; earned Social Influence is adjusted **once** only for a player's actual participation, never for an autonomous public event.

## A multigenerational society
- The organization-network graph, accumulated trust/competition, public-event history and surviving institutional names pass to descendants.
- At inheritance, all links become **dormant** with no leaders or active public invitations. Only when current-generation living NPCs renew the parent organizations do the intergroup links reactivate. Past personal friendships, alliances, jobs and finances are never copied onto strangers.

## Interface and game feel
- World: full intergroup network cards show two actual organizations, representative portraits, trust/competition bars, joint public-event progress, leadership transitions, archived outcomes and ancestral memories.
- People and Life: compact playable invitations and current-year feedback. Work: compact professional-network overlaps.
- Accessible player options with actual consequence measurements. Responsive layout targeted at 320, 375 and 430 pixels; no compulsory dialogs or recurring reminders.

## Save and QA
- Existing offline save schema version 2 remains compatible. New optional organizationNetwork is strictly validated and bounded to at most six unique intergroup links, one active decision, 24 completed event stories, 25 news items, ten ancestral records and eight history entries per link.
- New engine and end-to-end tests cover grounded pair formation, professional and rival ties, choice tradeoffs, neutral actions, later branches, autonomous gatherings, real leadership succession, dissolved groups, inherited dormant links, save validation, old saves, responsive mobile layouts, and accurate one-time player influence.
- The merge gate is the full npm engine regression suite, Expo dependency check, iOS/Android/web export, and Playwright browser gameplay tests on the final feature commit.

No store builds, submissions, native signing, or automations.
