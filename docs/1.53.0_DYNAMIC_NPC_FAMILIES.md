# 1.53.0 — Dynamic Families & Independent NPC Lives

Build 63 (iOS and Android). Depends on 1.52.0 / Build 62.

- Adults in the existing cast can marry independently. Two eligible friends can form a couple; otherwise a new, actual community NPC joins the cast. Player partners and known related pairs are excluded.
- Independent couples develop a relationship score, welcome children with stable person IDs, divorce, become widowed, or remarry. Their children use the same aging and career engine as other NPCs.
- Child expenses are debited from the parents' existing finances once per year (including after divorce), without touching the player's funds. Adults can switch to qualified careers; the family panel shows combined adult net worth and annual child expenses.
- Households form bounded affinity links, gather autonomously, and generate wedding, birth, anniversary or family gathering invitations through the existing two-offer-per-year inbox. The player can attend or decline under the usual activity/cost rules.
- Old saves need no migration. Simulation is annual, not real-time, capped at 24 generated people, 24 marriages, 32 household links and 40 news entries per life. Existing succession selects a new cast; independent household records do not persist across generation changes. This is an abstract family graph, not a full legal marriage, housing or custody simulator.
- Eight core tests and one browser regression are included. CI verification is required before merging.
