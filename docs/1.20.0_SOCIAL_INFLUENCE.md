# Lifely 1.20.0 — Reputation, Social Influence and Intergenerational Expectations (Build 30)

## Real outcomes become lasting reputation
- The new **social-influence engine** ingests existing, completed Competing Futures, NPC Ambitions, Social Crossroads, autonomous school-alumni and Story Director events. Each real story is recorded only once, using the original stable ID and actual decision/outcome.
- Three independent reputation circles distinguish **family trust**, **professional standing** and **community influence**. Helping one family member does not magically settle a business rivalry or earn a public accolade. Unanswered and neutral actions have no automatic reputation bonus.
- Every score change has an in-game evidence label, age, source and measured impact. Up to 40 recent signals are displayed, with retained event-identity protection to prevent repeatedly awarding the same source during save reloads or later years. No new random rolls.
- The existing global reputation stat makes a small, transparent contribution to effective professional standing; it is not replaced.

## Opportunities with actual people
- A high or damaged earned reputation can open **optional** conversations with living family members, colleagues or alumni already tracked in the NPC world. Professional meetings require an actual matching job/business connection, community initiatives require real community-involved acquaintances, and family callbacks require real adult relatives.
- Accepting an introduction or acknowledging past damage adjusts the NPC's real trust and rivalry, the player's relevant stat, and one standing score. Using leverage dismissively has different measurable consequences. Declining an invitation is neutral: it consumes the normal activity but changes no money, trust or reputation.
- Invitations expire without penalty and are capped at one active and one introduction every four years. NPC loss interrupts rather than reviving anyone. No promises of fictional salaries, clients, partners or deals, and no free assets or currencies.

## Long-term memory
- The People tab gives a three-meter reputation board, evidence log, NPC invitations and archived outcomes. The World tab surfaces the same influence system. The Life tab shows active/just-resolved opportunities without losing immediate feedback.
- Significant choices enter the existing Life Chronicle and inform Story Director callbacks.
- Generational inheritance retains ten compact ancestor summaries and a very modest **family-name expectation** (maximum +6/-6 per category). The heir starts with unearned scores at baseline and **does not** receive automatic NPC bonds, jobs, access to opportunities without living connections, or money.

## Compatibility and QA
- Offline save schema remains version 2, and socialInfluence is optional for old saves. Strict guards cover scores, event IDs, no duplicated evidence, phase/state transitions, null targets, histories and inheritance. Bounded state: 190 seen story IDs, 40 evidence items, 24 completed opportunities, 26 news, 10 ancestral notes.
- Tests cover authentic career alliances, conflicts and accountability, independent reputation circles, strictly neutral decisions, RNG/cash invariance, repeated yearly ticks and save reloads, missed invitations, real NPC absence, community crossroads, corrupted saves, inherited expectations, Life Chronicle, and mobile viewports 320/375/430px.
- Release gate: all core tests, Expo dependency check, iOS/Android/web export and complete Playwright browser suite must pass on the **final** commit before merging.

No scheduled automations, signing, TestFlight uploads, Play Console submissions or public releases performed.
