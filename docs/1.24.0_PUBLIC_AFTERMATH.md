# Lifely 1.24.0 — Consequential Public-Event Afterlives (Build 34)

## Long-term consequences spanning up to 18 years

The 1.23.0 playable events no longer end when the final ballot, public forum, festival, or competition closes. Consequential real-player outcomes launch persistent relationships in a new **Public Aftermath** subsystem:

- **Leadership and election losers:** An actual losing candidate and winning real NPC remember the election. Their rivalry can intensify independently; player mediation or support for opposition changes both NPCs and the actual group's intergroup reputation.
- **Former professional competitors:** Fair competitions can become collaborative professional networks; controversial results develop rivalries. Players with an actual career can establish a recorded joint project. Players who own a business can form a **named joint venture with a real consenting NPC**; the actual business partnership ledger records the contact and source event, with no fabricated income, equity, career promotions, cash or royalties.
- **Community festivals:** A successful festival can become a long-lived community partnership, while a divided celebration leaves deep group conflicts. Real NPC connections can carry consequences into another existing community or family organization. No new or imaginary member is created just to connect groups.
- **Public agreements:** Negotiated deals lead to continuing collaboration and contested terms lead to long-term feuds.

## Autonomous living world

- Every three in-game years, real NPCs and organizations remember the original event and develop their own bounded trust, rivalry, cohesion and intergroup relationship adjustments. Player cash, stats, jobs and earned influence do **not** change just because a former contact is thriving or feuding.
- At most five concurrent saga threads. Each concludes after up to 18 years, contact loss or institutional dissolution; outcomes are archived. Invitations return at reasonable intervals, remain optional and expire neutrally. They never block aging.
- Player choices are meaningful: repair the relationship, favor one side and escalate rivalry, or remain independent. Replies affect actual NPCs and shared organizational projects; spillover happens only through verifiable real common members. One normal yearly activity is required, with no special currency.
- An authored World board shows both real people, conflict/affinity meters, decades of milestones, the next return age and exactly what prior choices changed. Life, People and Work receive contextual compact panels.

## Existing-game integration

Significant follow-up outcomes appear in the **Life Chronicle** and may influence future **Story Director** episodes with the original participants. Earned Social Influence changes once per meaningful player response, with strict deduplication. Neutral, passive or unanswered interactions earn nothing.

## Generational safeguards

Institutional news and ancestral summaries remain accessible to heirs. A new generation does not inherit the original adult contacts, old NPC friendships, free partnership revenue, voter support, or active promises. Older version-2 saves load without the new state.

## Quality and release gate

- Bounded schema: 5 active sagas, 30 archive stories, 65 original event IDs, 12 milestones per saga, 25 news, 10 ancestral summaries, 6 optional decisions per saga; strict save validation.
- New engine regression tests cover event-to-saga mapping, actual election losers, contests and community history, autonomous three-year development, optional repair/escalation/neutral responses, business collaboration, professional projects, trust/rivalry changes, NPC death, 18-year closure, lossless old saves, inheritance, idempotent reputation and corruption prevention.
- Playwright tests cover World/People/Work interactions, save/reload, real NPC and project updates, independent options and 320/375/430px layout.
- Require **both** GitHub Actions engine and Expo/browser jobs green on the exact final PR revision before merging.

No TestFlight upload, Google Play submission, automations, or store releases are performed.
