# Lifely 1.28.0 — Living institutions

Existing organizations can develop independent two-year succession contests after at least five years of institutional leadership. Actual current adult members are candidates and voters; no invented ballots. A majority elects the challenger, and ties retain the incumbent. Players may endorse a candidate or remain neutral; a challenger victory changes an existing project mission and incurs transition friction. The same group leader ID is used by the organization, intergroup and civic systems. Historical outcomes survive the generational handoff, but no active candidates or memberships are inherited. Old saves initialize governance on annual evolution. Five engine tests and a browser test cover the new path. Structural splitting, merging and cross-group defections are not implemented in this release; they require changing the existing one-group-per-kind invariant.
