# Lifely 1.3.0 (build 12) — Everyday Gameplay QA

## Implemented
- Touch-first relationship board with two characters, trust/tension meters, three contextual decisions and NPC reactions. Choices are influenced by the character's hidden priorities, the current life statistics and a seeded unexpected complication. Bond, world trust/rivalry and person memory can all change.
- Classroom board with five distinct school challenge tracks, an ongoing named classmate, group decisions and score-dependent school results. Age 6–18; school play is limited to once per year.
- Fictional courtroom stage (only with an active case). Three rounds cover records, testimony, and closing statements; a preparation metric affects the existing fictional verdict model. No legal guidance.
- Relationship and school stories can be recalled one or two years later. The returning message can be answered once and costs one yearly activity, or ignored without blocking progression.
- Career modules from 1.2.0 are unchanged. No real-world legal, educational or medical process is claimed.

## Manual device tests still required
1. Play all five career projects and the new three everyday boards on small and large iPhones and Android devices; confirm no overflow or inaccessible controls.
2. From ages 6–18, play each school track, use the same classmate over several years, and verify that rewards and trust changes persist after force quitting.
3. Resolve relationship stories with supportive, adventurous and practical responses. Confirm different agendas can succeed and that repeated choices are not always optimal.
4. Trigger a fictional legal case. Complete a three-stage hearing, abandon a separate hearing, and use the original one-step hearing route. Verify cases are resolved exactly once and legal outcomes appear in the journal.
5. Force-close after each decision and restore from saved slots and backups. Verify continuing as a child inherits records but not active ancestor obligations.
6. Advance years through returned relationship/classroom memories, reply once and ignore others; no old messages should replay or block the age button.
7. Confirm original sound effects, mute settings, Reduce Motion, screen readers, touch target size, and no microphone permission. Check web/browser and actual iOS/Android separately.
8. Conduct human comparative playtests for entertainment and repeat engagement. No claim of outperforming BitLife until independently supported.

## Design boundaries
The courthouse is entirely fictional and no courtroom action is advice about representing oneself. School and relationship outcomes are simplified entertainment. App Store and Play Store releases still need signed build verification, device QA, age rating, accessibility audit and content review.
