# Lifely 1.26.0 — Mandate Delivery and Public Accountability

## Why this exists
A successful election, negotiation, or civic campaign is **a promise**, not proof of delivery. The existing eight-year organization campaign now creates an accountable six-year implementation arc after joint adoption, prioritized action or partial compromise. Gridlock and dissolved proposals do not produce fictional projects.

## Mechanics
- One delivery record per genuine concluded agreement, referencing the original two real groups and their actual existing intergroup link. Replacements can speak only if they are actual living group representatives. Unavailable organizations pause the record and eventually close it as disbanded.
- Every two in-game years the world independently updates delivered-work progress, verification and coordination using the original groups' real cohesion, tension, active project progress, trust and competition. **There is no simulated spending, revenue, fake project, free player stat gain or office assignment.**
- Player follow-up choices are optional, consume one of the usual yearly actions and have visible tradeoffs:
  - Evidence review: greater verification and trust, slight earned knowledge.
  - Push for delivery: faster progress but more tension and rivalry, slight earned confidence.
  - Respect independent implementation: a recorded neutral choice that cannot alter player stats, people, organizations, shared links or cash.
- At the deadline, the initiative is evaluated as delivered, partial or stalled using bounded progress, proof and coordination. If real partners cease to exist, it can instead end as disbanded.
- Group histories and Lifely news remember outcomes; family inheritance preserves only a condensed historical note, not living contacts, titles or policy powers.

## UI
World and contextual Life, People and Work panels display a separate **Promise vs Delivery** card showing three live meters, real participants, status, history, optional accountability decisions and post-deadline outcome. Compact previews only appear when there is a relevant decision or newly completed result.

## Compatibility and limits
- Still **version-2 offline saves**. A missing `civicMandates.deliveries` field from 1.25 is accepted and initialized on next gameplay tick.
- Maximum 12 delivery records; each retains 10 timeline entries and four player decisions.
- No new network services, external providers, subscriptions, automation jobs, signed builds or store submissions.

## Test gate
New engine and Playwright coverage addresses independent progression, optional choices, grounded trust/tension effects, deadline outcomes, neutral paths, old saves, hostile/invalid data, historical-only inheritance and 320px responsive behavior. Merge only when core and bundle/browser CI succeed on the **final PR head**.
