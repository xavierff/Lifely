# Lifely 1.6.0 (Build 16): Playable Hobby Leagues

## New original gameplay
- Eight disciplines: chess, street basketball, open-mic music, cooking, dance, indie game jam, photography and gardening.
- Every discipline has three authored tactical situations with three distinct responses each (24 situations, 72 response choices). The player sees a cue, makes a tactical decision, and receives immediate feedback. Correct tactics dominate outcomes; opponent skill, player attributes and persistent mastery affect performance.
- Practice and contest sessions use three rounds and cost only one annual activity. Practice builds mastery; competitions test progress against persistent rivals, with capped adult in-game prizes and fair opponent progression.
- Two persistent opponents per hobby gain ability autonomously as years pass. Rivals remember previous encounters. Each hobby tracks lifetime practice, wins/losses, mastery, current league, streak, championship points and trophies.
- Life screen: eight selectable hobby cards with a competition board, a clear progress view, opponent avatars, tactics and lessons, persistent result records and prize collection.
- One session per discipline per year; at most two hobby sessions across disciplines per year. No optional activity can trap year advancement if abandoned.
- Important ties: creative success can raise entertainment fame, competitive street basketball can boost sports form, an indie game jam can enhance the existing business quality statistic. A spectator from the player's circle gains a small trust/bond effect on success.
- All gameplay stays offline and original. Existing version-2 saves remain valid. Successor lives inherit historical trophies rather than an ancestor's unfinished contests or personal skill.
