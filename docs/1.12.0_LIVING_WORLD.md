# Lifely 1.12.0 — A Living Regional World

## Gameplay shipped in source
The world changes as you live. Six entirely fictional national market simulations have five independently fluctuating economic sector indexes: innovation/research, education/care, infrastructure/resources, culture/hospitality, and commerce/finance. Twenty individually authored headline templates affect local regional indexes periodically. The simulation is deterministic, offline, and does not claim to represent real countries' economies.

### Economic mechanisms
The player's current country and job industry determine an economic multiplier from 0.87 to 1.13 applied to regular-job salary. Ordinary reputation produces a separate bounded salary factor from 0.93 to 1.075. Reputation also modestly influences promotion readiness. Business sectors, property valuations and rental income are tied to corresponding market indexes, ensuring regional changes affect actual annual finances. All outcomes remain simulated and subject to market risk.

### Regional sagas
Five original sector-specific regional projects have three authored chapters each (15 scenes), all with three original choices. Start at any eligible adult age, then return after two and four years. Participation is voluntary. Collaborative, evidence-led and publicity-driven decisions cause meaningfully different changes to the economy, integrity, community standing, rival trust, stress, career experience and reputation. Ignored chapters expire with recorded unfinished consequences. No age advance with a ready and unresolved regional decision.

### Competitor houses
Three named rival houses exist independent of the player. Their standing and wins evolve each year without any player interaction. Player relationships can shift through direct collaboration, competition or reconciliation, with realistic in-game time and money costs. Their rivalry, standing and previous interactions remain within the same family when a child inherits the playable life, but ongoing projects and unfinished decisions are never automatically inherited.

### Ordinary actions
Volunteering, working, training, creating, improving a business, supporting other characters and reconciling existing rivals feed lasting public reputation. This integrates older gameplay choices with a new causal world rather than simply attaching a cosmetic meter.

### User experience
Life tab: regional sector ratings and market factors, headline, reputation, rival status and available projects. Active chapter appears above ordinary gameplay until resolved. Work tab: industry and reputation exposure. More tab: cross-generation world journal. Screens adjust to 320/375/430px viewports.

## Verification gates
- npm test: deterministic and bounded six-country indexes, complete mapping across all 150 careers, market-to-salary effects, age-gated saga callbacks, real choice consequences, autonomous competitor actions, older offline saves, intergenerational rival history, invalid save rejection.
- npx expo install --check
- npx expo export --platform all
- npm run test:e2e: browser interaction and save/reload, three-way choices, two-year callbacks, rival alliances, viewport regression.

## Still needed before public release or superiority claims
Independent comparative testing against the currently available BitLife experience, multiple 3+ generation human playthroughs, accessibility and touch-target reviews on real iOS/Android devices, visual and economy balance review, native suspend/resume and save migration QA, and a separately approved signed build. No scheduled automation was created, disabled, or modified.
