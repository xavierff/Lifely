# Lifely 1.9.0 — Family Dynasty Stories

## What changed
12 authored family plots, 3 original chapters each, 3 player choices per chapter, with named characters and persistent stakes:
- Partner: career versus shared life; rebuilding trust; retaining individuality
- Parenting: confidence and practice; school pressure; teen independence
- Siblings: cooperation and competition; fairness and adult family assets
- Care and identity: dignified elder support; family businesses; strained bonds; family traditions

Families no longer consist only of a bond number. NPC values are grounded in their existing personalities and ambitions. Children develop independence and personal care; sibling pairs retain trust, rivalry and cooperation. Choices cause actual changes in these profiles, the NPC financial/career-adjacent world, stress, happiness, social outcomes and subsequent generations. The authoring is fictional and is not legal, parenting or health advice.

Every story takes place in the current year, with a second scene two years later and a finale four years after the start. The player must choose from meaningful contrasting decisions before advancing a year, but is never forced to start a family storyline. Unanswered conversations expire; changed or deceased family members result in honest interrupted histories rather than fabricated completion.

2. Screen-reader and large-font usability on physical iOS and Android phones, including narrow-screen touch targets.
3. Editorial consent and care-scenario review; do not portray fictional events as personal guidance.
4. Measure engagement, attachment to named NPCs, non-repetitive choices and voluntary second-lifetime completion against a current BitLife baseline in matched playtests.
5. Confirm save migration, economic impacts and no duplicated awards under real suspend/resume conditions.
6. No signed/TestFlight/Google Play submissions without user consent.
